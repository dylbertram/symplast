Before the first click

A note about
first launch.

@@SIGNING_NOTICE@@

Drag Symplast to Applications, then open it from Applications or Spotlight. Look for its icon in the menu bar, not the Dock.

SSH targets need a working SSH key and agent. Symplast cannot prompt for a password or key passphrase. Quitting the app does not stop your Mutagen sessions.
